We derive exact finite-length fluorescence statistics for four combinatorial null models of amyloid fibrils: split-YFP alone, split-YFP with Sup35p, FRET YFP/CFP, and FRET YFP/CFP with Sup35p. In their natural units, the split-YFP statistics R_SY(n) = 2E(n)/n and R_SY,S(n) = 2E(n)/n converge to 2/3 and 1/3, while the FRET statistics R_F(n) = E(n)/n and R_F,S(n) = E(n)/n converge to 1/2 and 2/9. Because these ratio families represent different physical quantities, they should not be compared as fluorescence yields. For split-YFP systems, bivariate generating functions and transfer matrices give exact probability distributions P(n,k) as closed binomial sums and rational functions. They also yield closed forms for k times 2^k-weighted shallow diagonals of Pascal's triangle related to OEIS entries A127976 and A095977. A common rational recurrence structure unifies the Sup35p models and gives exact expectations and variances; independent Markov chain arguments verify the results. For FRET systems, linearity of expectation gives a finite interaction-radius baseline, with E_r(n)/n tending to 2r p_C p_Y under an additive unit-weight cutoff. We also give unequal-concentration limits and Monte Carlo spot checks. The models are transparent combinatorial baselines, not mechanistic descriptions of fibril growth.
